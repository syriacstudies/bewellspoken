Important. Well Spoken is a parent education and support resource. It does not provide clinical screenings, assessments, diagnoses, or treatment. All content is general information. Everything on this site is meant to support, not replace, a professional clinical treatment plan developed by a licensed speech-language pathologist or your child's healthcare team.

The Parent Concern Questionnaires on this site are not clinical screenings. They are a structured way for you to organize what you are noticing about your child's communication, so you can have a clearer conversation with a speech-language pathologist or your pediatrician. They do not produce a diagnosis and cannot replace an evaluation by a licensed professional.

All directory listings on this site are example profiles unless otherwise marked as verified. Availability and insurance information is subject to change and should be confirmed directly with each SLP. Well Spoken is not affiliated with the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association (ASHA).
