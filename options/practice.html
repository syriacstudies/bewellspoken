How Well Spoken works

Three steps. One person reading every word.

We are the soft first door before the clinic. We read, we write back, and we only refer on when it is actually time.
Step 01

You write a short note

Tell us in a few sentences what you are noticing. The age, the worry, how long you have been sitting with it. There is no form.

Step 02

Jill reads and writes back

Within one business day, you get a personal reply. A reading, a first practice, and an honest read on whether a real evaluation is the next step.

Step 03

A warm handoff, only if you need one

If a clinician makes sense, Jill personally matches you to someone in her Bergen County network. If one does not, she says so.

Common questions

The things parents ask before they write in.

Is this a speech therapy practice?
Not in the traditional sense. Well Spoken is a concierge reading and referral service. Jill reads what you send, gives you a real first take and some reading, and then, if it is time, matches you to an evaluator or therapist in her Bergen County network.
What does it cost?
The library, the check in, and your first note are free. Longer consultations and the at home program have a fee schedule we share before anything is booked. No insurance paperwork.
How quickly will I hear back?
Within one business day. If you write Monday morning, you will have a real reply by end of day Tuesday at the latest, almost always sooner.
My child is already in therapy. Is this still for me?
Yes. We work with plenty of families who want a second read or a warmer set of resources to use at home alongside their current clinician.
