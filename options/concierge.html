What this place is

Something small and personal, before the clinic or the long wait.

Well Spoken is not a therapy practice yet, and it is not an app. It is a concierge reading and referral service. You write in. I read. I respond with what I would do if you were my friend asking me at a birthday party.

If a real evaluation is the next step, I hand you to someone I know and trust in Bergen County. If it is not, I tell you that too, and I give you a few careful things to try at home first.

What happens next

From the moment you write, to the conversation on the other side.

First

You write the note

A few sentences is plenty. Age, what you are seeing, how long it has been on your mind.

Then

Jill reads it personally

Not a bot. Not an intake coordinator. Her, at her desk, with coffee, within a business day.

And then

A real first conversation

A reading, a small thing to try at home, and, if it is time, a warm introduction to an SLP she trusts.

Your neighborhood

Bergen County is small, and the right clinician is usually eight minutes away.

Jill grew up here, trained in New York, and built her network one coffee at a time. Pediatric SLPs, feeding specialists, and school-based clinicians she has personally worked alongside.

When a referral makes sense, it is a name, a short note of introduction, and a real human handoff. Not a search engine result.
