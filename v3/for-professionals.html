If you are a pediatrician, a teacher, a daycare or preschool director, or anyone else who hears "is this something?" from parents, this page explains what Well Spoken is and how to use it in your setting.

What this site is

Well Spoken is a free educational resource about children's speech and language development, written for parents and reviewed by a licensed, ASHA-certified speech-language pathologist. There is nothing to buy, no account to create, and no email capture. We do not diagnose, and every page points families back toward you and toward qualified local clinicians.

The three things you can hand a family

The milestones

Age-banded expectations from CDC, ASHA, and NIDCD guidance, with clear "when to check in" markers. Useful when a parent needs calibration rather than alarm.

bewellspoken.com/milestones

The check-in

An eight question parent questionnaire, by age band, that produces a printable summary of the parent's observations. Families can bring it to their next visit with you, which makes that conversation faster and more specific.

bewellspoken.com/check-in

The library

Plain-language guides to evaluations: what they include, school vs. private pathways, how to read a report, and when a second opinion makes sense.

bewellspoken.com/library

What the check-in is, and is not

The check-in is a structured way for a parent to organize observations. It is deliberately not a screening instrument, produces no scores, and never tells a family that their child is fine or not fine. Its three possible outputs are: skills appear within the typical range, some observations worth a professional conversation, or a recommendation to reach out to a pediatrician or SLP. The thresholds are intentionally conservative, and any report of regression routes straight to "talk to your pediatrician promptly."

We keep the pediatrician in the loop by design. The site consistently tells families that hearing checks, medical questions, and global developmental concerns belong with their physician first.

For practices and programs

If you would like a one page printable version of the milestones or the check-in for your waiting room or parent nights, or you have feedback on anything we have written, we would like to hear it. Clinical corrections are especially welcome and are reviewed by our SLP.
