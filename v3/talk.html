Talk to us

A short note is
a fine beginning.

If this site did not answer your question, or you just want to tell another human what you are noticing, write to us. We read everything, and we will point you somewhere useful.

A few sentences is plenty. Consider including:
  • Your child's age
  • What you are noticing, in your own words
  • How long it has been on your mind
  • Anything a teacher, pediatrician, or relative has said about it
  • Where you are located, if you are hoping for local suggestions

Write to hello@bewellspoken.com

A note about what this is: we are a small family project, not a clinic, and a reply is information and direction, not medical advice or a diagnosis. For anything urgent or medical, call your pediatrician.

If you have not yet, the five minute check-in is a good thing to do before writing. You can copy the summary it produces straight into your note.
