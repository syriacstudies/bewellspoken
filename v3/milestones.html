Milestones by age

Signposts, not deadlines.

These are the things most children do by a given age, drawn from CDC, ASHA, and NIDCD guidance and reviewed by a licensed speech-language pathologist. The timeline varies more than parenting books sometimes suggest. Use these to get your bearings, not to grade your child.

Seeing a few things on the "when to check in" list? The next step is calm and small: take the five minute check-in and bring the summary to your pediatrician or a speech-language pathologist. None of these lists are diagnoses. They are reasons to have a conversation.
