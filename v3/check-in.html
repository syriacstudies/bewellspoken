The check-in

Eight questions about
what you are noticing.

Five minutes, give or take. No email, no account. Your answers stay on this device and you can print or copy a summary at the end.

Before you start, the honest fine print. This is not a clinical screening, assessment, or diagnosis. It is a structured way to organize what you are noticing about your child's communication, so you can have a clearer conversation with your pediatrician or a speech-language pathologist if you decide to. The goal is to help you feel prepared, not to give you a label.
